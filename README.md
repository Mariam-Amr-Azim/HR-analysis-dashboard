# HR-analysis-dashboard
An interactive Human Resources dashboard created using Microsoft Excel to analyze employee demographics, salary distribution, departmental income, and employee attrition.

# Questions-KPIs:
- Employee demographics: What is the average, maximum, and minimum employee age?
- Salary analysis: What are the average hourly rate and average, maximum, and minimum monthly incomes?
- Attrition by job role: Which job roles experience the highest employee attrition?
- Attrition by age band: Which age groups are more likely to leave the company?
- Departmental income: How does monthly income differ across HR, Sales, and R&D?
- Income by job role: Which job roles have the highest and lowest average monthly incomes?
- Gender distribution: How are employees distributed between male and female employees?
- Workforce age distribution: Which age groups represent the largest proportion of the workforce?

# Key Insights:
- Employee attrition: Sales Representatives have the highest attrition rate, followed by Laboratory Technicians. These roles may require further investigation to identify retention challenges.

- Attrition by age: Employees under 25 have the highest attrition rate, Employees aged 25–34 also show a relatively high rate, suggesting that younger employees may need additional support and career development opportunities.

- Monthly income by department: Sales has the highest average monthly income at approximately 6,950, followed by HR at 6,655 and R&D at 6,300.

- Monthly income by job role: Managers and Research Directors receive the highest average monthly incomes, while Sales Representatives, Laboratory Technicians, and Research Scientists have considerably lower averages.

- Gender distribution: The workforce contains more male employees than female employees.

- Age distribution: Employees aged 25–34 represent the largest age group, followed by those aged 35–44. This indicates that the workforce is largely concentrated in younger and middle-career age groups.

# Final Conclusion
The Human Resources dashboard provides an overview of employee demographics, compensation, departmental income, and attrition patterns. The analysis highlights younger employees and specific job roles, particularly Sales Representatives and Laboratory Technicians, as priority areas for further investigation.

The HR department could use these findings to improve employee retention through career development programs, employee satisfaction initiatives, and better opportunities for professional growth.
